## Map data

Every map is generated by the generator in [`tools/src/mapgen`](tools/src/mapgen): the Earth maps from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), free vector and raster map data in the public domain, and the city maps from satellite land cover and elevation. Each map's manifest names its sources again.

| Map                    | Dataset                                                                                                                                                 | Layers                                                                                                        | License                                       |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| World                  | [Natural Earth 5.1.2](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/releases/tag/v5.1.2), 1:10m                                                       | land, lakes, glaciated areas, geography regions and elevation points, bathymetry, populated places, countries | Public domain                                 |
| Europe                 | [Natural Earth 5.1.2](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/releases/tag/v5.1.2), 1:10m                                                       | same as World                                                                                                 | Public domain                                 |
| Americas, Africa, Asia | [Natural Earth 5.1.2](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/releases/tag/v5.1.2), 1:10m                                                       | same as World                                                                                                 | Public domain                                 |
| Island, Strait, Ring   | Drawn for Frontfall in their definitions                                                                                                                | none                                                                                                          | MIT                                           |
| Paris, Lyon            | [ESA WorldCover 10 m 2021 v200](https://esa-worldcover.org/)                                                                                            | land cover: open water and tree cover                                                                         | CC BY 4.0                                     |
| Paris, Lyon            | [Copernicus DEM GLO-30](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM) | elevation                                                                                                     | Copernicus DEM GLO-30 licence, free of charge |

- Produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved. The organisations in charge of the Copernicus programme by law or by delegation do not incur any liability for any use of the Copernicus WorldDEM-30.

The names of the city maps' nations are those of the arrondissements, quarters and neighbouring towns they start in; their starting points were placed by hand at each town hall.

The files and their checksums are listed in [`tools/mapgen/sources.json`](tools/mapgen/sources.json). See [docs/maps.md](docs/maps.md) for how they are used.

## Artwork

The logo, icons and brand artwork were drawn for Frontfall. The files are MIT like the rest of the repository; the Frontfall name and logo still identify the official project, see [Name and logo use](docs/brand.md#name-and-logo-use).

Game design is inspired by browser territory games such as [OpenFront](https://openfront.io/). No code, assets or text from those games are used.
