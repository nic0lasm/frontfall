Privacy policy

Frontfall is a free game. As a guest it knows nothing about you. If you sign in, it keeps a name, a scrambled id and your achievements, and nothing else. Last updated 28 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Frontfall is a personal, non-commercial project of Nicolas M, who runs the public server at nclsmgr.com/frontfall. Questions, access and erasure requests: the contact form of the site, or GitHub issues (public). The legal notice of the site names its publisher and host.

If you play as a guest

No account, no email, no advertising, no tracker. The name you type is shown to the other players of your game and forgotten when the game ends. Games exist in the server's memory only. The game's anonymous reports (below) go to its own server and nowhere else.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional; it keeps your name and your achievements for you. You can sign in with Discord or Google, or with a link sent to your email address.

With Discord or Google, the game asks Discord only who you are (identify) and Google only your profile (openid profile), once, at sign-in. With email, the server sends one message to the address you type, with a link that works once and for 15 minutes; the address serves for that message only. The server keeps:

  • the name you play under, which starts as your first or display name at the service, or the name in the menu when you sign in by email;
  • your id at that service, or your email address, scrambled with a random key kept on the server (HMAC-SHA256), so that it finds your account next time but the file alone does not say who you are;
  • how you signed in, and the day the account was made and last used;
  • your achievements and the counts behind them, so that they follow you to another device.

It never keeps your email address in clear, nor your picture or your contacts, and forgets the service's token at once. Your address is not written in the game's logs; the link's code is kept only as a hash, in memory, until it is used or expires. The message goes out through the site's mailbox at OVH, in the European Union. This is used to recognise you and to keep your name and achievements yours, and for nothing else. It is kept until you delete your account. Your games and reports are never linked to it.

To delete your account, open the menu and choose Delete my account under your name: it is erased from the server at once, with its achievements, and your name becomes free again. If you cannot, ask through the contact form. Discord and Google keep their own data about you, under their own policies.

Cookies and your browser

One cookie, frontfall_session, set only if you sign in: it keeps you signed in for 30 days, holds your account number and is used for nothing else. Signing out ends it everywhere. It is strictly necessary, so there is no banner.

The game also keeps in your browser, for you alone: your name and last settings, your language, your profile and achievements, and your seat in an online game so that a reload brings you back. None of it is sent to the server, except your achievements once you sign in. Forget my profile and achievements in the menu erases them from the browser, as does clearing the site's data; those of your account come back when you sign in again.

Anonymous counters

To know which maps and modes people use and where they give up, the page sends a short report at the end of a game, when an error happens and when you vote on the end screen. Each report holds only coarse facts (map, mode, device kind, length in ranges, how it ended), never a name or an address; the server adds one to a few counters and forgets the report. A browser that sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control sends nothing. The full list of fields is in the technical page and the counters are public at /api/stats.

Reports and bans

If you report a player in an online game, the server keeps the report for 30 days for its operator to read: time, game, reason (one of four), the reported player's name and, for both players, a reference drawn from the network address with a secret key. The address itself is not written down. The operator may ban a network address or such a reference to keep away someone who abuses others.

Logs, hosting and backups

  • The game's own logs never hold an address in clear, only a reference that changes at each restart. The web server in front of the game keeps technical logs, which include network addresses, for 30 days, to detect and block abuse.
  • The server is at OVH, in Gravelines, France, in the European Union, so your data does not leave the Union on its way to the game. The traffic goes through Cloudflare, which protects the site and speeds it up.
  • The game's data (counters, accounts, reports) is backed up every night in encrypted form to Cloudflare R2, in the European Union. Backups are kept three months at most, so an erased account also leaves them within that time.

Your rights

You may ask for access to your data, its correction or erasure, or object to its use, with the contact form. If the answer does not satisfy you, you may complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority. This text changes only to follow the game; the date at the top says when.
