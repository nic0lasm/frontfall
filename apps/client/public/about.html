Conquer a world with no edges

Frontfall is a free real-time strategy game that runs in your browser. Start from a single tile, outgrow your neighbours and hold the map. Rounds are short, there is nothing to install and no account to make.

Play now

How to play

  1. Pick where you start. Click any land tile when the game opens.
  2. Expand. Click land next to yours to take it, or a neighbour's land to attack. Keys 1 to 9 set how much of your troops each attack uses, 0 sends them all. On a phone, use the slider and tap.
  3. Build. Gold comes in by itself. Spend it on cities (more troops), forts (harder to take), ports (trade by sea) and missile silos or air defenses.
  4. Cross the sea. Click a coast across the water to send a ship, through the Panama and Suez canals too.
  5. Deal with others. Ally, trade, send gifts and quick messages, or betray. You can also play in teams.
  6. Win. Hold 75 % of the land, be the last one standing, or have the most land when time runs out.

Press H or the ? button in a game to see this help again, with the keys and the cost of each building.

Where and with whom

  • Solo against bots, at three difficulties.
  • Online in a public game, or in a private game you share with an invitation link.
  • Eleven maps: the World, Europe, the Americas, Africa and Asia drawn from real data, and six quick maps.
  • In English and French, with a keyboard mode, accessibility settings and touch controls.
  • Play as a guest, or sign in with Discord, Google or email to keep your name and achievements.

Beta, and open source

Frontfall is in beta until version 1.0, which puts the world on a 3D globe: rules and visuals still change. It is free to play and never pay-to-win. The code is under the MIT license, so you can read it, fork it and run your own server with one Docker image. It is written from scratch; the game is inspired by browser territory games, and no code, asset or text from them is used.
